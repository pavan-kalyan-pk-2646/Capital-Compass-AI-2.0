# Lending and Affordability

## LA-1 FOIR ceiling
Total EMIs (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio, FOIR) above 50% of take-home income are treated as unaffordable. A FOIR at or below 40% is the comfort level used by Capital Compass.

## LA-2 Total cost disclosure
Every loan analysis must disclose the EMI, total interest, processing fee and total repayment over the full tenure.

## LA-3 Rate risk
Floating-rate loans must be stress-tested for a rate rise of at least 2.5 percentage points over their remaining tenure.

## LA-4 Consumption debt with a thin emergency fund
Taking unsecured consumption debt while the emergency fund covers fewer than 3 months of essentials and EMIs is not advisable.

## LA-5 Simulation only
Capital Compass analyses affordability and consequences. It never submits, links to, or pre-fills real loan applications.
