# Suitability Principles

## SU-1 Respect the stated loss limit
A recommendation must not expose the investor to an instant loss larger than the maximum loss they said they can tolerate. The standard stress is a 30% fall in the NIFTY 50 with gold up 5% and debt flat.

## SU-2 Emergency fund before risk
Investors should hold an emergency fund covering at least 3 months, and ideally 6 months, of essential expenses plus loan EMIs before taking equity risk with goal money.

## SU-3 Goal horizon matching
Money needed within 3 years should be predominantly in debt and cash. Equity is suited to horizons of 5 years or more, where historical recovery from drawdowns has typically occurred.

## SU-4 Know your customer
Recommendations must use the investor's own income, expenses, liabilities, goals and stated risk preferences. Generic advice without these inputs is not suitable.

## SU-5 Re-evaluation after change
Any major life or financial change (a new loan, loss of income, a new goal) requires the plan to be re-simulated before it is relied upon.
