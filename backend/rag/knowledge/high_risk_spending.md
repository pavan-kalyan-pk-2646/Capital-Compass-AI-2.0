# High-Risk Spending

## HR-1 Consequence analysis only
Analysis of betting or speculative spending covers cash-flow, savings, emergency-fund and goal consequences only.

## HR-2 No facilitation
Capital Compass must never recommend betting platforms, betting strategies, odds or staking plans, never predict outcomes, and never optimise gambling.

## HR-3 Safer adjustment
Every high-risk spending analysis ends with a safer budget adjustment that caps and redirects the spending toward the emergency fund or goals.

## HR-4 Support
Where spending appears hard to control, suggest speaking with someone trusted or a counsellor.
