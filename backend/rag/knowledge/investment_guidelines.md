# Investment Guidelines

## IG-1 Diversification
A recommended portfolio must hold at least three asset classes (for example equity, debt and gold). Single-asset portfolios concentrate risk and are not suitable as a default recommendation.

## IG-2 Equity ceiling by age
Equity exposure should generally not exceed (100 − age)% plus a 10-point margin, and never more than 85% of investable assets. Investors above 50 years old should keep equity below 70%.

## IG-3 High-risk ceiling
Even for aggressive profiles, equity should not exceed 80–85% of investable assets. Small-cap exposure should stay a minority of the equity sleeve.

## IG-4 Cash drag
Cash held inside the investable portfolio (excluding the emergency fund) should not exceed 20%. The emergency fund is held separately and is not counted as investable cash.

## IG-5 Product categories, not products
Recommendations are expressed as product categories (index fund, short-duration debt fund, gold ETF). Naming specific schemes, guaranteeing returns or promising outcomes is not permitted.
