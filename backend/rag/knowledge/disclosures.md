# Disclosures

## DS-1 Scenario-based analysis
Capital Compass provides scenario-based financial decision support. Simulated outcomes are not guaranteed predictions and should not be interpreted as regulated personalized financial advice.

## DS-2 Past performance
Simulations use historical NIFTY 50 and Indian debt returns plus stated assumptions. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

## DS-3 Model limits
Gold returns, inflation and the correlation model for individual holdings are assumptions, documented in the Trust Center.

## DS-4 Human decision
The investor remains responsible for the final decision and may wish to consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
